Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026. App by TapBudget.

TapBudget does not collect your data. Your budgets stay on your device, and there is no account.

What the app stores

The app stores what you enter: budget names, bill and income names, amounts, due days, paid dates and the amounts you spent. It also stores your settings, such as each budget’s colors and wallpaper, light or dark mode, whether bill reminders are on and whether the lock is on. All of this stays on your device. The iPhone app also keeps a second copy in its own storage on the device, so a budget can be restored if the phone clears the first one.

Wallpaper pictures

If you choose a picture as a wallpaper, the app makes a smaller copy of it and keeps that copy in storage on your device. The picture is never uploaded, and it is not added to share links or backup files.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up and no login. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your budgets to us or to anyone else.

Bill reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app asks iOS for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and delivered by iOS. They are not sent from a server. They show the bill name, not the amount.

Face ID lock

If you turn on the lock, the app asks iOS to check Face ID or your passcode. iOS does the check. The app never receives or stores your face data or fingerprint.

Sharing and backups

You choose when to share or save a copy. A share link contains the budget itself, and your paid history too if you pick that option. Anyone who has the link can read it, so send it only to people you trust. A backup file is saved where you choose. We do not see links or backup files.

The web version

The same app also runs in a web browser at tapbudget-ashy.vercel.app. That page is hosted by Vercel, which may keep its own technical logs, such as your IP address, under Vercel’s own policy. The app does not send your budgets there.

Deleting your data

In the app, open the menu, choose Manage budgets and delete a budget. Deleting a budget also deletes its wallpaper picture, unless another budget uses the same one. To remove a wallpaper on its own, open the menu, choose Wallpaper and colors and tap Remove.

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. In a browser, clearing the site’s data does the same. Links and backup files you shared stay with the people who have them.

Children

The app is not aimed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
