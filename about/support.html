Find your way around

  • Tap the app icon at the top left to open the menu, or swipe in from the left edge of the screen.
  • The round + at the top right adds a bill, a paycheck or an everyday budget.
  • Tap the budget name under the greeting to switch budgets.
  • On the Overview, swipe the cards left or right, or tap Bills, Money in or Spending above them.

Mark a bill paid

Switch the bill on. Today’s date fills in. If you paid on another day or a different amount, change the date and the amount right under the bill.

Tap the round arrow at the end of a bill to open its own page. You can switch it on there too, and swipe through the bills coming up next.

Move a budget to another phone

Nothing syncs on its own. To move a budget:

  1. Open the menu and choose Share this budget. Pick Share setup, or Share with paid history. Copy the link.
  2. On the other phone, open TapBudget, open the menu and choose Import a budget. Paste the link and confirm.

On iPhone, copy the link rather than tapping it. A tapped link opens the web version in Safari, which keeps its own separate data. You can also save a backup file from the menu and restore it on the other phone.

Wallpaper pictures and colors don’t travel with a link or a backup file. Set them again on the other phone.

Bill reminders

  • Open the menu and choose Turn on bill reminders. Allow notifications when iOS asks.
  • You get a note at 9:00 the day before a bill is due and on the day it is due.
  • Marking a bill paid removes its reminder. The example budget has no reminders.
  • If nothing arrives, open the iPhone Settings app, choose TapBudget, then Notifications, and make sure they are allowed.

Face ID lock

Open the menu and choose Turn on Face ID lock. The app asks for Face ID when it opens, and when you come back after a short time away. If Face ID does not work, use your phone passcode. Turning the lock off also needs Face ID or your passcode.

Keep a backup

Your budgets live only on your device. If you delete the app or reset the phone, they can be lost. Open the menu and choose Save a backup file now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe. To bring a budget back, choose Restore from a file.

Wallpaper and colors

Open the menu and choose Wallpaper and colors. Each budget keeps its own wallpaper, colors and look, and the sheet tells you which budget you are changing.

  • Tap Choose a picture to use one of your photos. Dim fades it and Blur softens it. Remove takes it away.
  • Pick a ready-made color set, or choose your own three colors.
  • Light, dark and automatic are in the menu too. They apply to every budget.

If a photo won’t open, try saving it as a JPEG or PNG first.

If something looks out of date

Close TapBudget completely and open it again. It picks up the newest version the next time it opens with a connection.

Delete your data

Open the menu, choose Manage budgets, and delete a budget. Deleting the app removes its data from your phone.
