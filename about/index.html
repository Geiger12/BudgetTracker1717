Everything for the month on one phone

Bills, money in and everyday spending, with the date filled in for you.

Tick off bills

Switch a bill on when you pay it and today’s date fills in. If you paid a different amount or on another day, change it.

See the month at a glance

Swipe through three cards: bills, money in and spending. A health light turns amber when something is due soon and red when a bill is overdue.

Open any bill

Each bill has its own page. Swipe through what’s coming up next and switch those on too.

Everyday budgets

Set an amount for groceries, gas or eating out, and enter what you’ve spent as you go.

Where the money goes

Planned against paid for each item, a six-month chart and a simple projection of your loan and card balances.

Bills that pay themselves

Mark a bill as automatic and it switches itself on when it’s due. Loans and credit cards get their own progress bars.

Share or back up

Send a budget as a link, with or without what you’ve paid, or save a backup file.

Make each budget your own

Keep one budget for the household and another for a trip or a side job. Each keeps its own wallpaper, colors and look, so you can tell them apart at a glance.

  • Use a photo from your phone as the wallpaper. It stays on your phone.
  • Pick one of six color sets, or choose your own three colors.
  • Light, dark, or follow your phone.
  • Text stays readable whatever picture or colors you choose.

Your budget stays on your phone

No account

There’s nothing to sign up for and no password to remember.

No server

Budgets are saved in the browser on your phone. They don’t leave it unless you share them.

Links you control

A shared link carries the budget inside it. Anyone with the link can read it, so delete the message once it’s been imported.

Keep a passcode on

TapBudget doesn’t encrypt what it saves, so keep a passcode on your phone.

Add it to your iPhone

TapBudget runs in Safari. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app.

  1. Open it in Safari

    Go to TapBudget in Safari.

  2. Add to Home Screen

    Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

  3. Open it from the icon

    Use the new icon from now on. Safari keeps its own copy of your data, separate from the Home Screen app.

Questions

What if I get a new phone?

Open the menu and save a backup file, then restore it on the new phone. Wallpaper pictures aren’t in backups, so add yours again.

Can two of us use the same budget?

One of you shares it as a link and the other imports it. Each phone then has its own copy. Changes on one phone don’t show up on the other.

Does it need the internet?

After the first visit, TapBudget keeps a copy of itself on your phone so it can open and save without a connection.

Can I keep more than one budget?

Yes. Create them from the menu, and switch between them by tapping the budget name under the greeting.
