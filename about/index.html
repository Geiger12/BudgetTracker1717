Budget Tracker is a simple budget tracker that lives on your phone. There is no account, and your budget stays on your device.

Open the app How to add it to your Home Screen

Four tabs along the bottom

Everything is a thumb away. The Overview fits on one screen, so the gauges are never cut off.

Overview
Three gauges and a short summary, all on one fixed screen.
Bills
Income and bills, with filters and a weekly schedule.
Spending
Everyday budgets like groceries, and what is left of each.
Insights
Expected income, where the money goes, and a six-month chart.

Tap a bill paid

Switch a bill on when you pay it. Today’s date fills in for you. If you paid on another day, or a different amount, change it right there.

  • Bills due soon are tagged, and late ones turn red.
  • Everyday spending has its own box: type what you have spent so far.

Filter to what’s left

Choose All, Unpaid, Paid or Overdue. Each one shows how many items it holds, so you can see what still needs paying at a glance.

  • A red badge on the Bills tab counts overdue bills.
  • The app remembers the filter you chose.

Plan the week

Switch Bills from List to Schedule to see every bill and paycheck by week of the month. The current week is outlined, and the colors show what is paid, due soon or overdue.

Make it look like yours

Pick one of six ready-made color sets, or choose your own three colors. Text colors adjust so everything stays easy to read. Use light, dark, or follow your phone.

Share it, or keep a copy

Send your setup, or your whole budget with its paid history, as a link. The other person gets a confirmation before anything is added. You can also save a backup file from the menu.

On iPhone, copy the link rather than tapping it, then import it from inside the app.

  1. Open the link in SafariUse the Open the app button on this page.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Where is my budget stored?

On your device. Clearing your browser’s website data, or deleting the app, can erase your budgets, so save a backup file from the menu before big changes.

Can I use it on more than one phone?

Not automatically, because nothing is synced. Use Share with paid history, or a backup file, to move a budget to another phone.

Who can see my budget?

Only people you send a link or a backup file to. A shared link contains the budget itself, so send it to people you trust and delete the message afterwards.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Open the menu, then Colors. Choose a ready-made set or pick your own, and switch between light, dark and automatic.
